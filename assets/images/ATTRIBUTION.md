# Image attributions

Every bundled image must be listed here with its source and licence (design-system.md §11), and credited in the app (Settings → Image credits, driven by `photoCredits` in `lib/app/theme/app_imagery.dart`; keep both in sync).

Accepted licences: own photos, CC0/public domain, CC BY, and CC BY-SA. For CC BY-SA, the cropped versions here are adaptations and are themselves licensed CC BY-SA 4.0; this does not affect the app's code.

All current screen photos come from one real rooftop garden (shade net, terracotta pots on a concrete terrace), photographed by **Suresh Khole**, licensed **CC BY-SA 4.0**, from the Wikimedia Commons series "Roof Top Garden" (accessed 2026-10-18).
