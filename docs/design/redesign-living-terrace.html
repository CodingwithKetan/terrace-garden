Design direction C

Your terrace,
alive on screen.

The app opens on a drawing of the user's own terrace — their pots, their plants, seen from above. Everything else hangs off that picture.

  1. 1Plants, not tasks. Work appears on the plant that needs it — a water-drop pin on the tomato — before it appears in a list.
  2. 2Drawn, not iconified. Every species has a botanical illustration, top view and side view. No icon-in-a-circle anywhere.
  3. 3Honest time. A plant's life is shown only through sourced milestones — sown, transplant, harvest window — never invented stages.
  4. 4Quiet surfaces. Limestone ground, hairlines, one card style. Colour is reserved for plants, pots and the one thing to do now.
